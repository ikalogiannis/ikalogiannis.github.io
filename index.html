I am broadly interested in deep learning theory, particularly in using analytic tools to understand the behavior of deep neural networks. I received my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens, where I was advised by Petros Maragos and worked closely with Nikos Tsilivis.

 

Research interests: Deep Learning Theory, Statistical Physics of Machine Learning, Optimization

My Diploma thesis, Initialization and Optimization of Neural Networks with Ranking Layers, studies the trainability of deep morphological/ranking/linear networks through mean-field signal propagation analysis.
